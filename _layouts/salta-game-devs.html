A community crafting games, from code to art

We are a collaborative community of video game development and design in the province of Salta, Argentina — bringing together creative, technical and curious people to learn, create and share experiences around the world of video games.

We aim to become a leading community in Latin America, transforming game development into a tool of innovation, education and cultural growth while positioning Salta as a creative and collaborative hub of global impact. Like the projects we ship, we grow on shared passion, reliable collaboration and a love for the craft.
