Gorfus el Redentor is a platforming and action scroller inspired by the classic arcades of the '80s and early '90s. You advance from left to right, jumping over traps and striking enemies with a sacred staff, facing a boss at the end of each stage. No handed-out checkpoints and no infinite lives: every hit counts, every jump matters.
