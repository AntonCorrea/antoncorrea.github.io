A solo puzzle game where three influential women from history — Eva, Joan of Arc, and Marie Curie — are destined to die. You travel between their eras and solve puzzles to alter their fates. The core mechanic is contextual puzzle solving that blends historical facts, the role of women in history, free will, and a bit of humor.
